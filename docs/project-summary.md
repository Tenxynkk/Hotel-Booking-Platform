Hotel Booking System — Project Summary
Project Title

Hotel Booking System

Domain

Hospitality

Problem Statement

Hotels may rely on manual or disconnected processes to manage rooms, customers, reservations, check-in/check-out, and payment records. Such processes can lead to inaccurate room availability, duplicate bookings, inefficient record management, and difficulty accessing important information. Customers may also experience difficulties when searching for rooms and making reservations.

The proposed Hotel Booking System will provide a centralized web-based platform for managing hotel operations and reservations.

Objective

The main objective of the project is to develop a web-based Hotel Booking System using the MERN Stack that simplifies hotel operations by managing rooms, customers, bookings, check-in/check-out, payments, and related information through a centralized and user-friendly platform.
